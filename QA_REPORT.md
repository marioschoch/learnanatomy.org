## 2. Findings, ordered by severity

### C1 — CRITICAL — Most of the "right" vagus nerve lies on the patient's LEFT and is labelled "Right" in the app
- **What I saw.** I selected `vagus-nerve-x-r` and isolated it with the left vagus, oesophagus, trachea and aortic arch (anterior view).
  - In the neck the highlighted nerve runs on the trachea's right, which is correct.
  - Below the aortic arch it crosses to the patient's left of the oesophagus and fans out over the left upper abdomen.
  - The 3D label "Nervus vagus (X) **R**" points to a nerve segment on the patient's left (screen right).
  - The info panel says "Side: Right (patient's view)".
- **Measured** on the decoded GLB geometry (QA-12, x > 0 = patient's left):

  | band | `vagus-nerve-x-r` mean x [range] | `vagus-nerve-x-l` mean x |
  |---|---|---|
  | neck y 1.40–1.55 m | −0.020 [−0.028 … −0.008] ✓ | +0.021 |
  | upper thorax 1.30–1.40 | −0.009 [−0.019 … +0.011] ✓ | +0.013 |
  | lower thorax 1.15–1.30 | **+0.039 [+0.012 … +0.070]** (2 504 vertices) ✗ | — (left nerve ends at ≈1.30 m) |
  | abdomen < 1.15 | **+0.038 [−0.015 … +0.069]**, z = +0.026 (anterior) ✗ | — |

  The left nerve stops in the upper thorax. Below that, the "right" nerve takes the course of the left vagus and anterior vagal trunk: left of the oesophagus, then onto the anterior stomach. This looks like an upstream modelling or labelling mix-up, not a plausible right-vagus course.
- **Status.** Known and accepted by the owner (ARCHITECTURE.md §12.15, `overrides.json` 'accepted', validator warning). Per PROMPT §1 ("a swapped left/right … is a critical bug") I still report it as critical, because the user is shown the wrong side for most of the structure.
- **Repro.** Open `/?s=vagus-nerve-x-r&l=skeletal.100,visceral.100,nervous.100&i=i:vagus-nerve-x-r,vagus-nerve-x-l,oesophagus,trachea,aortic-arch`, then choose the anterior view.
- **Evidence.** `desktop-qa12-vagus-anterior-right-selected.png`, `desktop-qa12-vagus-posterior-right-selected.png`, `tests/e2e/qa-anatomy.spec.ts` QA-12.
- **Needs** an anatomist's decision. Options for the lead (not done by QA): show no side for this structure, split it, or swap the parts.

### C2 — CRITICAL (licence) — The deployed site omits upstream attributions that Z-Anatomy lists as "included and adapted"
- **What's missing.** The in-app credits (About dialog) list Z-Anatomy, BodyParts3D, Wikipedia, Dundee Inner Ear (NC) and Cowley Kidney (NC). They do **not** list:
  - "Cranial Nerves and Foramina – University of Dundee, CAHID – **CC BY 4.0**"
  - "Brainder" and "White matter" (University of Washington)
- **Why it matters.** The upstream README lists these models as "used as reference/included and adapted", and per-structure provenance is unknown. CC BY 4.0 §3(a) therefore requires the attribution wherever the derived models are distributed.
- **Why the site has no fallback.** ATTRIBUTION.md, which does contain them, is neither deployed nor linked (see H2). So the attribution appears nowhere on the site.
- **Severity.** Classified critical (licence breach) under the conservative reading. Downgrade only if the owner confirms that no distributed geometry derives from these models.
- **Repro.** Open the app, About (phone: Menu → About), then search the dialog for "Cranial Nerves": no match.
- **Evidence.** `desktop-qa10-about.png`, QA-10 soft check.

### H1 — HIGH — Phone quiz: the highlighted target is hidden behind the quiz sheet (Identify and Name it)
- **What happens.** For Identify and Name-it questions the camera centres the target on the canvas (`focus`). On the phone the quiz bottom sheet covers the canvas centre:
  - quiz panel at y 389–771 px; canvas centre at (195, 422) px, i.e. inside the panel (measured in QA-08b).
  - On desktop the panel (x 1045–1427) does not cover the centre (720, 450).
- **Effect.** The student sees only ghosted context and has to guess, or pan the camera manually.
- **What I saw.**
  - Identify: only the top edge of the orange carpal bone peeks out above the sheet (`phone-qa08b-identify-question.png`).
  - Name it: the highlighted toe phalanx is not visible at all (`phone-qa08c-name-typo-feedback.png`).
- **The same geometry** applies when the phone info sheet or Tools sheet is open after "Focus".
- **Repro.** Phone width: Menu → Quiz → keep only "Identify" → Start.

### H2 — HIGH — Full attribution and licence texts are not reachable from the deployed site
- **What happens.** The credits say "The full attribution is in ATTRIBUTION.md in the project repository", but there is no link (`src/config.ts` `APP_REPO_URL = ''`). `GET /ATTRIBUTION.md`, `/LICENSE` and `/assets.LICENSE` on the built site return the SPA's `index.html` (`text/html`), because only `public/` is deployed.
- **Effect.** The modification notice, the NC structure list and the licence texts that go with the downloadable `/models/*.glb` and `*.json` are not available on the site.
- **Requirement not met.** PROMPT §6-C.9 asks for "full attribution from ATTRIBUTION.md" on the credits page.
- **Repro.** `npm run build && npx vite preview`, then `curl -I localhost:4173/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- **Evidence.** QA-10 soft checks.

### M2 — MEDIUM — The liver is modelled twice; explode and the Locate quiz expose it
- **The data.** `liver` (10 506 triangles) and its 8 Couinaud segments (`parentId: liver`, I–VIII, together ≈ 90 % of the liver's bbox volume) are all visible by default. Unexploded, the segments are hidden inside the liver: clicking the liver selects `liver`, which is correct.
- **Consequences:**
  - **Explode > 0.** The segments move out of the liver on their own offsets, so duplicate liver tissue appears. Clicking the focused, exploded liver selects "Segmentum mediale sinistrum hepatis (IV)" (`desktop-qa07-click-liver-exploded.png`, `phone-qa07-click-liver-exploded.png`).
  - **Locate quiz.** The 8 segments are in the default pool (8 of 396 structures). They cannot be clicked, because the liver encloses them, nothing is hidden during Locate, and the tree is disabled in quiz mode. I saw "Segmentum posterius hepatis (I)" asked as a Locate question.
- **Repro.** Default view → Explode 60 % → focus the liver → click its centre.

### Observations (not defects)
- The info panel always titles the node with its Latin name (PROMPT §6-C.5). The name-language toggle changes the 3D label, the phone peek, search and quiz, but not the info-panel title.
- `vite preview` gzips JS/CSS/JSON but sends GLB (`model/gltf-binary`) uncompressed. Meshopt GLBs shrink by another ≈ 33 % with gzip (skeletal 2.15 → 1.43 MB), so it is worth checking whether Cloudflare Pages compresses this content type. The startup budget is met either way.
- A Locate answer by a real 3D click at the target's bbox centre is not a reliable test for curved or enclosed targets: rib → centre is inside the thorax; palatine bone → inside the skull. Picking itself is verified in QA-07 (click on the focused liver selects `liver`).
